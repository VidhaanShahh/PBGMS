1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) describes the functional and nonfunctional requirements
for Release 1.0 of the Pet Boarding and Grooming Management System (PBGMS). The document is
intended to be used by the members of the project team who will design, implement, and verify the
correct functioning of the system, as well as by instructors and reviewers evaluating the project. Unless
otherwise noted, all requirements specified here are considered high priority and committed for Release
1.0.

1.2 Document Conventions
This document follows the IEEE 830 / Karl Wiegers SRS template format. Functional requirements are
labeled using a dotted naming convention (e.g., Booking.Create, Grooming.Schedule) so that each
requirement can be uniquely referenced and traced. Priority levels (High, Medium, Low) are noted in
parentheses where a requirement is not automatically high priority. Business rules are referenced as
BR-n and are summarized in Section 5.5.

1.3 Intended Audience and Reading Suggestions
●​ Project team members (analysts, developers, testers) — read the entire document.
●​ Course instructors / evaluators — Sections 1, 2, and 3 provide the best overview of project scope
and functionality.
●​ Developers — focus on Section 3 (System Features) and Section 4 (External Interface
Requirements) and Appendix A (Data Model).
●​ Testers — use Section 3's functional requirements and Section 5's nonfunctional requirements to
derive test cases.
●​ Project managers — Sections 1.4, 2, and 5.4 summarize scope, users, and quality attributes.

1.4 Product Scope
The Pet Boarding and Grooming Management System is a web and mobile-accessible application that
allows pet owners to register their pets, book boarding stays and grooming appointments online, track
booking status, and make payments. The system also allows the pet care business (front-desk staff,
groomers, kennel attendants, and administrators) to manage bookings, assign staff and kennel resources,
record service history, process payments, and generate operational reports. The goal of the system is to
replace manual, phone-based, and paper-based scheduling with an integrated digital solution that
reduces double-booking, improves customer communication, and gives management visibility into
occupancy and revenue.
Business objectives:
●​ Reduce scheduling conflicts and missed appointments caused by manual booking.
●​ Provide pet owners with 24/7 self-service booking and booking status visibility.
●​ Give staff a single system to manage kennel occupancy, grooming schedules, and pet care
instructions.
●​ Automate payment collection and provide management with occupancy and revenue reports.

2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective
The PBGMS is a new, self-contained system that replaces the current manual process of taking boarding
and grooming bookings by phone, walk-in, or paper appointment book. It is a web application with a
responsive interface usable from desktop browsers and mobile browsers, backed by a central database
and business logic tier. Figure 1 (Context Diagram, described below) illustrates the external entities that
interact with the system in Release 1.0. The system is expected to evolve in later releases to integrate
with veterinary record systems, SMS gateways from additional providers, and payment processors
beyond the initial choice.

2.3 User Classes and Characteristics
User Class

Characteristics

Pet Owner (favored)

A customer of the pet care business who registers pets and books boarding
or grooming services. Pet Owners are expected to be the most frequent
users of the system; an estimated majority of bookings will be created online
rather than by phone or walk-in. Pet Owners may manage multiple pets
under one account and may need to provide vaccination records and
behavioral notes. Technical proficiency is assumed to be that of a typical
smartphone/web user; no specialized training is required.

Front Desk Staff

Employees who handle walk-in customers, check pets in and out, take
payments, and answer phone bookings by entering them into the system on
the customer's behalf. Front Desk Staff require basic computer/web-browser
training on the system.

Groomer

Staff who perform grooming services. Groomers primarily view their
daily/weekly schedule, see pet care notes, and update the status of a
grooming appointment (e.g., in progress, completed).

Boarding/Kennel
Attendant

Staff responsible for the daily care of boarded pets. Attendants view kennel
assignments and feeding/medication instructions, and record daily care
activity.

Administrator (Manager)

Business owner or manager responsible for configuring services and pricing,
managing staff accounts and schedules, managing kennel/room inventory,
and reviewing operational and financial reports. Administrators require full
access to configuration and reporting features.

System Administrator

Technical staff (or the development team, in early releases) responsible for
user account management, system configuration, backups, and monitoring.
This role is typically filled by IT personnel or a super-admin account.

OE-1: The PBGMS shall operate correctly with current versions (as of the release date) of Google
Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari on desktop and mobile devices.
OE-2: The PBGMS server components shall operate on a Linux-based server running a supported
web application runtime (e.g., Node.js or equivalent) and a relational database engine.
OE-3: The PBGMS shall be accessible over the public Internet via HTTPS, as well as from an internal
network at the pet care facility for staff workstations.
OE-4: The system shall support access from mobile browsers on iOS and Android devices without
requiring a native application install for Release 1.0.

2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints
CO-1: The system's design, code, and documentation shall conform to the project team's coding and
documentation standards (to be defined by the team, analogous to a corporate development
standard).
CO-2: The system shall use a relational database management system for persistent storage of pets,
bookings, staff, and payment records.
CO-3: All web pages shall conform to HTML5 and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines where
feasible.
CO-4: Payment card data shall not be stored directly by the PBGMS; the system shall delegate card
handling to a PCI-DSS-compliant third-party payment gateway.

2.6 User Documentation
UD-1: The system shall provide online help (FAQ and how-to guides) accessible from every page
describing how to book, modify, and cancel boarding and grooming appointments.
UD-2: The first time a new Pet Owner logs in, the system shall offer a short guided walkthrough of
the booking process.

2.7 Assumptions and Dependencies
AS-1: The pet care facility operates on defined business days and hours during which boarding
check-in/out and grooming appointments are available; these hours are configurable by an
Administrator.
AS-2: Pet Owners have access to a valid e-mail address and/or mobile phone number for receiving
notifications.
DE-1: The operation of the PBGMS depends on the availability of a third-party payment gateway to
process online and card payments.
DE-2: The operation of the PBGMS depends on the availability of an e-mail/SMS provider to deliver
notifications.

3. System Features
3.1 Manage Pet Owner Account and Pet Profiles
3.1.1 Description and Priority
Allows a Pet Owner to create an account, and to add, view, update, or remove profiles for each of their
pets, including species, breed, age, weight, vaccination records, feeding schedule, medication needs, and
behavioral notes. Priority = High.

3.1.2 Stimulus/Response Sequences
●​ Stimulus: New user requests to register an account. Response: System collects owner contact
details and creates the account after verifying the e-mail address.
●​ Stimulus: Pet Owner requests to add a pet. Response: System collects pet details and vaccination
proof, and stores the pet profile linked to the owner's account.
●​ Stimulus: Pet Owner requests to edit or remove a pet profile. Response: System updates or
removes the profile, provided the pet has no active or future bookings when removal is
requested.

3.1.3 Functional Requirements
Requirement ID

Description

Account.Register

The system shall allow a new Pet Owner to create an account using name,
e-mail address, phone number, and password.

Account.Verify

The system shall send a verification e-mail and shall not allow bookings to be
placed until the e-mail address is verified.

Account.Login

The system shall allow a registered Pet Owner to log in using e-mail and
password, and shall lock the account for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed
attempts.

Pet.Add

The system shall allow a logged-in Pet Owner to add a pet profile including
name, species, breed, date of birth, weight, sex, and a photo (optional).

Pet.Vaccination

The system shall require the Pet Owner to provide proof of up-to-date core
vaccinations (or upload a document) before the pet is eligible for a boarding
booking, per BR-1.

Pet.Notes

The system shall allow the Pet Owner to enter feeding instructions, medication
instructions, and behavioral notes (e.g., anxiety, aggression, allergies) for each
pet.

Pet.Edit

The system shall allow the Pet Owner to edit an existing pet profile at any time.

Pet.Delete

The system shall allow the Pet Owner to remove a pet profile only if the pet has
no active, in-progress, or future bookings.

Pet.View

The system shall allow the Pet Owner to view a list of all pets registered under
their account and each pet's booking history.

3.2 Book Boarding Service
3.2.1 Description and Priority
A verified Pet Owner may book a boarding stay for one or more of their pets for a specified date range,
subject to kennel/room availability. The Pet Owner may cancel or modify a boarding booking prior to
check-in. Priority = High.

3.2.2 Stimulus/Response Sequences
●​ Stimulus: Pet Owner requests to book boarding for a pet. Response: System prompts for check-in
date, check-out date, kennel/room type preference, and special care instructions, then checks
availability.
●​ Stimulus: Pet Owner requests to modify a boarding booking. Response: If the booking status is
"Pending" or "Confirmed" and check-in has not occurred, the system allows the Pet Owner to
change dates or cancel, subject to availability.
●​ Stimulus: Pet Owner requests to cancel a boarding booking. Response: If the booking has not yet
reached "Checked-in" status, the system cancels the booking and applies the cancellation policy
(BR-4) to any prepayment.

3.2.3 Functional Requirements

The system shall verify that each selected pet has current vaccination records

Boarding.Dates

The system shall prompt the Pet Owner for a check-in date/time and a

Boarding.Instructions

saved profile notes.

3.4 View, Modify, and Cancel Bookings
3.4.1 Description and Priority
Pet Owners need a consolidated view of all upcoming and past boarding and grooming bookings, with
the ability to drill into details, modify, or cancel eligible bookings. Priority = High.

3.4.2 Functional Requirements
Requirement ID

Description

Bookings.List

The system shall display a list of the Pet Owner's upcoming bookings (boarding
and grooming) sorted by date, and a separate list of past bookings for the
previous 12 months.

Bookings.Detail

The system shall allow the Pet Owner to view full details of any booking,
including status, cost, payment status, and any staff notes visible to customers.

Bookings.Status

The system shall display the current status of each booking (e.g., Pending,
Confirmed, Checked-in, In Service, Completed, Cancelled), per the state model
in Appendix B.

Bookings.OwnOnly

The system shall permit a Pet Owner to view and manage only their own
bookings, not those of other Pet Owners, per SE-6.

3.5 Check-in and Check-out Management
3.5.1 Description and Priority
Front Desk Staff process the arrival (check-in) and departure (check-out) of boarded pets and the
start/completion of grooming appointments, updating booking status and recording any exceptions (e.g.,
late arrival, additional services requested on site). Priority = High.

3.5.2 Stimulus/Response Sequences
●​ Stimulus: Front Desk Staff searches for a booking by owner name, pet name, or booking number
at arrival. Response: System displays the booking and prompts staff to confirm check-in.
●​ Stimulus: Front Desk Staff confirms check-in. Response: System assigns/confirms the kennel or
room, sets booking status to "Checked-in," and records the actual arrival time.
●​ Stimulus: Front Desk Staff processes departure. Response: System calculates final charges
(including any on-site add-ons), processes remaining payment, and sets booking status to
"Completed."

of which drive the availability calculations used during booking. Priority = Medium.

3.7 Process Payments
3.7.1 Description and Priority
The system collects payment for boarding and grooming bookings, either online at time of booking
(deposit or full payment) or in person at check-in/check-out, via an integrated third-party payment
gateway. Priority = High.

3.7.2 Functional Requirements
Requirement ID

Description

The system shall allow a Pet Owner to select a payment method (credit/debit

payment method or select "pay in person" if permitted.

Payment.Refund

The system shall allow Front Desk Staff or an Administrator to issue a full or
partial refund to the original payment method when a booking is cancelled

4. External Interface Requirements
4.1 User Interfaces
UI-1: The PBGMS shall provide a responsive web interface usable on desktop and mobile browsers
without loss of core functionality.
UI-2: The system shall provide a context-sensitive help link on every page.
UI-3: All booking workflows shall be completable using the keyboard alone, in addition to

4.2 Hardware Interfaces
No mandatory specialized hardware interfaces have been identified for Release 1.0. Optionally, Front
Desk Staff workstations may use a barcode/QR scanner to look up a booking via a printed or emailed
booking confirmation, but this is not required for core functionality.

4.3 Software Interfaces
SI-1: Payment Gateway — the PBGMS shall communicate with a third-party payment gateway
through its programmatic API to: (a) submit payment authorization requests, (b) receive
authorization responses, and (c) submit refund requests.
SI-2: Notification Service — the PBGMS shall communicate with an e-mail delivery service and an
SMS gateway through programmatic interfaces to send booking confirmations, reminders, and status
notifications.
SI-3: Database — the PBGMS shall use a relational database management system through a
standard data-access layer (e.g., an ORM or SQL driver) for all persistent data.

4.4 Communications Interfaces
CI-1: The system shall send an e-mail and/or SMS message to the Pet Owner confirming the
acceptance of a booking, including price and relevant instructions.
CI-2: The system shall send an e-mail and/or SMS message to the Pet Owner reporting any problem
with a booking (e.g., payment failure, cancellation by the business) after the booking is confirmed.
CI-3: All communications with the payment gateway shall occur over an encrypted (TLS) connection.

5. Other Nonfunctional Requirements
5.1 Performance Requirements
PE-1: The system shall support at least 100 concurrent users during peak booking hours (estimated
6:00 pm–9:00 pm local time) without degradation of response time beyond the limits below.
PE-2: All web pages generated by the system shall be fully loaded within 3 seconds under normal
broadband conditions.
PE-3: Availability search results (boarding rooms or grooming slots) shall be returned within 5
seconds of the query being submitted.
PE-4: The system shall display booking confirmation messages within 4 seconds of the user
submitting a confirmed booking.

5.2 Safety Requirements
No direct safety-of-life requirements have been identified for the software itself. However, the system
shall reliably surface pet medical/behavioral notes (e.g., allergies, aggression) to staff at check-in and
during service to reduce the risk of mishandling, per SF-1 below.
SF-1: The system shall display a pet's medical alerts and behavioral notes prominently on the
check-in, kennel assignment, and grooming service screens.

5.3 Security Requirements
SE-1: All network transactions that involve payment information or personally identifiable
information shall be encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
SE-2: Users shall be required to log in for all operations except browsing publicly available service
listings and prices.
SE-3: Passwords shall be stored using a salted, one-way cryptographic hash; the system shall never
store passwords in plain text.
SE-4: The system shall enforce role-based access control so that Groomers, Boarding Attendants,
Front Desk Staff, and Administrators can access only the functions appropriate to their role.
SE-5: The system shall permit a Pet Owner to view and manage only their own pets and bookings,
not those of other Pet Owners.
SE-6: The system shall not store full payment card numbers; card data shall be handled exclusively
by the PCI-DSS-compliant payment gateway.

5.4 Software Quality Attributes
Availability-1: The PBGMS shall be available to online users 99.5% of the time measured monthly,
excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 24 hours in advance.
Usability-1: A first-time Pet Owner shall be able to complete a boarding or grooming booking
without external assistance in under 5 minutes, as measured by usability testing.
Maintainability-1: The system's business rules (pricing, cancellation policy, notification timing) shall
be configurable by an Administrator without requiring a code change or redeployment.

Scalability-1: The system's architecture shall support adding additional kennel/room inventory, staff,

Robustness-1: If the connection between the user and the system is interrupted before a booking is

5.5 Business Rules
BR-1: A pet must have current core vaccination records on file before it is eligible for a boarding
booking. Grooming bookings do not require vaccination verification but may be declined at check-in
for visible signs of illness, per facility policy.
BR-2: A boarding booking may not be created for a check-in date more than 12 months in the
future.
BR-3: A single online transaction may include bookings for multiple pets belonging to the same
owner, provided each pet meets its own eligibility requirements.
BR-4: Cancellation policy: a boarding or grooming booking cancelled at least 48 hours before
check-in/appointment time is fully refundable; cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a
cancellation fee equal to one night's boarding rate or the grooming service price, as applicable.
BR-5: Prices are calculated using the room type's daily rate (boarding) or the service's list price
(grooming) in effect at the time the booking is confirmed, plus any selected add-ons.
BR-6: A deposit of 20% of the estimated total (or 100%, at the business's configuration) is required
at the time of online booking; the remaining balance, if any, is due at check-in or check-out.
BR-7: Reminder notifications are sent 24 hours before the scheduled check-in date or grooming
appointment time, unless the Pet Owner has opted out of reminders.
BR-8: A late check-out fee applies if a boarded pet is picked up more than 1 hour after the facility's
configured check-out time, at a rate configurable by the Administrator.
